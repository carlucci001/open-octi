INITIALIZING…
PINCH (thumb + index) & MOVE — rotate
OPEN PALM — disassemble
FIST — reassemble
TWO PINCHES, PULL APART — zoom
G or top-right button — tracking on / off
NO CAMERA? drag with mouse, wheel to zoom, E to explode

Gesture Lab

Webcam hand tracking runs entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded anywhere. Pinch to grab the drone and turn it. Open your palm to pull it apart. Make a fist to put it back together.

Allow camera access when asked. Good light helps.
