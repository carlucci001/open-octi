# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

Effective date: {{effective_date}}

This agreement is between {{business_name}} and {{counterparty_name}}. Each party may disclose confidential business, technical, financial, or customer information for {{purpose}}.

Each receiving party will use confidential information only for that purpose, protect it with reasonable care, and share it only with people who need it and are bound by similar duties. Confidential information excludes information already lawfully known, public through no breach, received lawfully from another source, or independently developed.

If disclosure is legally required, the receiving party will give prompt notice when permitted. On request, confidential materials will be returned or destroyed, except for archival copies required by law. These duties continue for three years after disclosure; trade-secret duties continue while the information remains a trade secret.
