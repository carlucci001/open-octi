# Command Vault

This is your OpenOcti Markdown workspace. Your notes stay in this installation’s persistent data directory.

## Getting started

Open [[Guides/command-vault|Command Vault guide]], browse the Guides folder, or create your own note. Use `[[note name]]` links to connect ideas in the graph.

The included material is the public starter library. It does not contain another installation’s accounts, credentials, customer records, or private notes. Updates add missing starter files without overwriting your edits.
