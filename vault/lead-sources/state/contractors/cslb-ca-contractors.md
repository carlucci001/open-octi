Official CSLB statewide public files. The License Master CSV is joined to the current/most-recent Workers' Compensation CSV on `LicenseNo`. The portal also offers Personnel, but this source deliberately does not ingest it. The portal states that its files cover currently renewed and expired-but-renewable licenses, are free, and are current as of the date displayed beside each download. Observed 2026-09-04: both downloadable source dates were 9/4/2026.

The source contains business contact data, license status, issue/expiration dates, classifications, bonds, and workers' compensation coverage; it does not contain email addresses. License status may change after a bulk snapshot, so the CSLB Instant License Check remains the authoritative point-in-time verification surface.

Outreach is restricted to manual business-line calls and mail. Autodialed, prerecorded, or AI-voice outreach is refused unless recorded prior express consent exists on the lead.
