# Code of Conduct

OpenOcti project spaces are for respectful, useful collaboration.

- Be direct and kind.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, or publish another person's private information.
- Assume good intent while accepting correction.
- Keep technical discussions relevant to the project.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior harms the community. Report conduct concerns through GitHub private vulnerability reporting when privacy is needed.

This policy applies in the repository, issue tracker, discussions, pull requests, and project-related public spaces.
